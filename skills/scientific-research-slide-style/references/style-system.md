# Style System

This reference summarizes the visual rules extracted from the downloaded research template decks.

## Shared Profile

- Canvas: 20 x 11.25 inches, 16:9 widescreen.
- Common slide count: 20 to 36 slides.
- Common body text size: 16 pt.
- Common section/title sizes: 60 to 70 pt, with occasional 80 to 100 pt cover titles.
- Common support sizes: 20, 24, 25, and 30 pt for labels, card titles, and callouts.
- Common geometries: ellipses first, then rectangles and rounded rectangles. Many slides use 40 to 110 shapes.
- Content density: low to medium. A slide usually has 1 large title, 2 to 6 short content blocks, and 1 dominant diagram/card/image placeholder.

## Typography

Keep one font family per track:

- Dark tech scientific: Arial or Helvetica.
- Space science: Open Sans with Space Mono accents.
- Bright research: Lato or Montserrat.
- Medical pastel: Onest with Lexend accents.
- Editorial proposal: DM Sans or Inter.

If exact fonts are unavailable, use modern sans-serif substitutes, but keep the scale and weight. Body is usually regular 16 pt. Titles are bold, black, or extra-bold.

## Palette Tracks

### Dark Tech Scientific

- Background: deep navy or near-black navy such as `#000034`, `#000014`, `#08284F`, `#013057`.
- Accents: cyan `#2FA2E9`, blue `#00B2E3`, green `#19BD6F`, teal `#19B3AE`, pale blue `#C0E3F8`.
- Text: white or very pale blue on dark slides.
- Decorative layer: low-contrast circuit lines, dots, glows, thin geometry strokes.

### Bright Research

- Background: white, off-white, or bright cyan/teal cover fields.
- Accents: teal/cyan `#00BBD6`, blue-teal `#237194`, orange/yellow `#FAA32B`, `#F7C135`.
- Text: teal heading plus dark gray body.
- Decorative layer: molecules, atom icons, tiny dots, rounded cards, abstract corner blobs.

### Medical Pastel

- Background: pale blue fields and soft blobs.
- Accents: navy, teal, medium blue, mint.
- Text: dark navy headings, teal emphasized words.
- Decorative layer: plus signs, small dot grids, rounded white cards, subtle shadows.

### Editorial Proposal

- Background: warm neutral, sage green, salmon, black, beige, and off-white.
- Text: black or white high-contrast editorial type.
- Decorative layer: large full-bleed color panels, sparse columns, page numbers, small captions.

## Layout Grammar

- Covers: left or center image placeholder, very large title, one small label or button, decorative background.
- Table of contents: 4 to 6 equal modules, small numeric badges, light icon or image placeholder.
- Intro/objective slides: one large heading on one side, 2 to 4 short paragraphs or bullets on the other.
- Methodology slides: radial center object, numbered steps, timeline, or 2-column process cards.
- Findings/data slides: 2 to 4 cards, large number badges, simple icon above or inside each card.
- Problem/solution slides: stacked rounded cards or side-by-side color blocks.
- Closing/section slides: one large word/phrase with a single shape or image placeholder.

## Shape Rules

- Use circles and ellipses liberally for visual anchors, statistics, icon backgrounds, and abstract decoration.
- Use rounded rectangles for cards and content containers. Keep corners modest, not pill-shaped unless the source slide already does this.
- Use thin outline rectangles, hexagons, snipped rectangles, or large outlined frames on dark tech slides.
- Use pale blobs behind medical and bright research content, usually in corners or along slide edges.
- Use small repeated particles: dot grids, plus signs, molecule rings, star points, or circuit nodes.

## Exact Matching

For "giong y het", "same as the templates", or "make it like the downloaded slides":

1. Copy the closest reference PPTX.
2. Duplicate source slides that match the needed content pattern.
3. Replace text and images in-place.
4. Keep title positions, decorative objects, page numbers, and card sizes.
5. Only delete elements when the new content would overlap or become unreadable.
6. Render previews and compare to the contact sheet for that source deck.

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

- Starting from a blank white deck and only copying colors.
- Mixing the editorial proposal palette with the dark tech circuit style.
- Shrinking body text below 12 pt to force too much content.
- Replacing geometric placeholders with unrelated stock images.
- Removing small decorative shapes that establish the template rhythm.
- Using broad gradients, 3D mockups, or marketing landing-page composition.
