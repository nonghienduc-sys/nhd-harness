- `scientific-presentation-template_non_animated.pptx`
  - Best for: scientific overview, biology, chemistry, lab research, technology-heavy research.
  - Look: dark navy tech background, circuit traces, cyan/green accents, large white image placeholders, circles and hexagons.
  - Use when: the deck should feel advanced, digital, lab-oriented, or high-tech.

- `space-science-technology-presentation_non_animated.pptx`
  - Best for: astronomy, physics, engineering, space, aerospace, advanced technology.

  - Use when: the topic benefits from a futuristic or space-science mood.

- `research-presentation-powerpoint-google-slides_non_animated.pptx`
  - Best for: general research reports, methods, objectives, findings, data summaries.
  - Look: bright cyan/teal, white work surface, orange accent, rounded cards, molecule/atom motifs.
  - Use when: the output should be clear, friendly, and modern.

- `free-research-presentation_non_animated.pptx`
  - Best for: general research deck with stronger color blocks and metrics.
  - Look: teal/cyan cover, rounded frames, data cards, clean objective/methodology layouts.
  - Use when: the content needs more presentation energy than a formal proposal.

- `free-medical-collaboration-research-presentation_non_animated.pptx`
  - Best for: health, medicine, clinical research, collaboration, public health.
  - Look: pale blue blob backgrounds, navy/teal text, plus signs, dotted accents, white cards with soft shadows.
  - Use when: the deck should feel clinical, calm, and collaborative.

- `free-research-proposal-presentation_non_animated.pptx`
  - Best for: research proposals, dissertation planning, academic reporting.
  - Look: muted beige/sage/salmon/black editorial layouts, heavy uppercase titles, simple columns.
  - Use when: the presentation should feel formal, academic, and print-like.
