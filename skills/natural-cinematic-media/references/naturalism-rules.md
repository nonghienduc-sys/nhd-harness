# Naturalism and Anti-Slop Rules

Use these as a selection library, not a checklist to paste wholesale.

## Camera evidence

A convincing shot normally implies:
- a camera position and distance;
- one dominant focal-length/depth behavior;
- a plausible exposure/light source relationship;
- a reason for motion blur or lack of it;
- depth cues such as foreground overlap, atmospheric falloff, focus separation or scale.

Generic "cinematic" wording without these signals is weak.

## Material evidence

Prefer specific surface behavior:
- fur: directional strands, clumping, undercoat density, rim transmission only where backlit;
- skin: pores and fine tonal variation without exaggerated hyper-detail;
- cloth: weave, folds driven by gravity, compression at joints;
- metal: roughness variation, scratches/wear where handled, environment reflections;
- wood/stone: irregular grain/fracture, dirt accumulation, dampness in believable areas;
- foliage: variation in size, orientation, translucency and wind response.

## Contact and weight

Check:
- feet/hooves/paws actually meet the ground;
- shadows begin at contact areas;
- heavy subjects compress grass/mud or displace dust appropriately;
- poses show believable center of mass;
- moving subjects show anticipation, acceleration and follow-through.

## Natural imperfection menu

Choose 1–4 when motivated:
- fine organic grain;
- tiny exposure drift;
- slight lens breathing during refocus;
- gentle corner softness;
- subtle halation around strong practical highlights;
- low-amplitude handheld sway;
- partial foreground occlusion;
- non-uniform haze;
- small motion blur on fast extremities;
- dirt, wear, scratches, uneven fibers;
- slightly imperfect framing;
- subdued saturation.

Avoid combining heavy grain, strong vignette, chromatic aberration, bloom, flare, shake and blur all at once unless intentionally emulating damaged footage.

## Lighting discipline

Name the source and consequence:
- morning sun through thin cloud -> broad warm key, cool ambient fill;
- forest canopy -> broken top light, green bounce kept restrained;
- torch/fire -> warm local falloff, moving highlights, darker background;
- emissive magic -> nearby surfaces receive colored spill proportional to distance.

Do not use rim light as decoration when there is no plausible backlight.

## Fantasy grounding

Magic should interact with the physical scene:
- mist reveals beams;
- sparks illuminate nearby fur/foliage;
- wind changes particle trajectories;
- wet surfaces reflect bright magical sources;
- glow intensity falls off with distance;
- magical objects cast or occlude light.

## Common synthetic signatures to suppress

- waxy/plastic material;
- repeated microtexture across unrelated surfaces;
- every edge outlined by glow;
- perfectly clean symmetrical layouts;
- identical leaves/rocks/cloud shapes;
- floating feet;
- impossible fingers/limbs/horns/wings;
- bokeh pasted uniformly across depth;
- excessive HDR/local contrast;
- random blue/cyan light unrelated to the art direction;
- uniform particle density across foreground and background.
